The design documents live with the code, in the Kito repository:

- [ADRs](https://github.com/KitoOrg/kito/tree/main/docs/adr): the decisions
  that change the data model, the on-disk format, or a choice of the plan.
- [Decisions](https://github.com/KitoOrg/kito/blob/main/docs/decisions.md):
  every other choice made along the way, with its reason.
- [Phases](https://github.com/KitoOrg/kito/blob/main/docs/phases.md): what
  each phase of the project built, how it was tested, and its limits.
- [Demos](https://github.com/KitoOrg/kito/tree/main/docs/demo): the output
  of each phase's demo script.
